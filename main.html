Lifey × Jordan Breast Cancer Program

A feminine care brand has permission to talk about women's health that most sponsors don't. We used it — tying 10% of Lifey product value to funding early-detection screening for women who couldn't afford it, then carrying the same idea across three very different rooms.

الكشف المبكر ينقذ الحياة

  1. The Mechanic

    10% of Lifey product value directed to the Jordan Breast Cancer Program to fund screening for women unable to pay for it. A purchase became a contribution without asking the shopper to do anything extra.

  2. The Rooms

    Corporate — a staffed booth at King Hussein Business Park. Sport — sponsorship of the Think Pink padel tournament. Retail — the same messaging on pharmacy and supermarket collateral.

  3. The Partner

    Running under the Jordan Breast Cancer Program and King Hussein Cancer Foundation gave the campaign credibility a brand cannot manufacture alone.

Why it worked Cause campaigns fail when the brand is the hero. Here the brand was the funding route and the programme was the message — which is also why the creative stayed simple enough to survive a booth, a banner and a sponsor board without losing anything.
Confirmed Impact Direct screening subsidies unlocked for women across Jordan through pharmacy and retail product contributions during Breast Cancer Awareness Month.
Out of Home · Agency Side

Hongqi — A Number Before an Explanation

Launching a Chinese luxury sedan into a market that didn't know the badge. Rather than lead with the brand, we led with a number nobody could parse — then answered it on the next board.

  1. Teaser Phase

    ٤٣١ كيلو بدينار — "431 kilos per dinar". Weight per dinar is not how anyone shops for a car, which is exactly why it stopped people. No offer, no brand giveaway.

  2. Reveal Phase

    امتلك الفخامة — own the luxury. 26,000 JOD, or 431 JOD a month with no down payment. The teaser number turns out to be the monthly instalment.

  3. Integrated Platform

    #THE_LUXURY_WITHIN carried the idea into digital channels, dealer activations, and automotive press material.

My role & execution Concept development, strategic narrative, and bilingual Arabic copy on the GHS account team, delivering seamless alignment from Amman OOH boards to showroom floor.
Sports Marketing · Experiential & Sponsorship

40Love × 973 Padel — Experience the Elite of the Padel World

Transforming a 4-day tournament into Jordan's premier sports-lifestyle festival. In partnership with 973 Padel, we designed an integrated commercial strategy that unlocked tier-one corporate sponsors (Jordan Kuwait Bank and Orange Telecom), packaged cross-border sports tourism for UAE visitors, deployed an aggressive 3-phase rollout roadmap, and curated high-reach digital creators.

عِش نخبة عالم البادل — تحويل البطولة الرياضية إلى وجهة أسلوب حياة متكاملة

“A sports tournament is won inside the four glass walls, but a sports brand is built in the culture. By fusing athletic competition with deep house music, curated hospitality packages for UAE visitors, and tier-one corporate backing, we created an event people didn't just watch — they belonged to.”

  1. Phase 1: The Tease & Mystery Reveal (Days 1–3)

    Stretched over three days to build suspense: introducing the 40Love brand narrative, dropping social teasers, revealing the headline partnership with 973 Padel, and previewing prize pools to prime the market before registration opened.

  2. Phase 2: Omnichannel Scale & Influencer Blitz (Days 4–17)

    A 13-day conversion surge to lock in 50–100 tournament entries. Deployed high-frequency Meta paid ads, street billboards, and mobi-screens across Amman. Ran parallel weekly story flights with selected digital creators (Ahmad Al Shayiip, Abdullah Tahhan, Life As Sara) reaching 450K–600K targeted regional viewers.

  3. Phase 3: Sponsor Lock-In & Tournament Countdown (Days 17–21)

    Secured headline commercial underwriting from Jordan Kuwait Bank and Orange, paired with local F&B businesses providing value-in-kind services. Activated a real-time countdown with early-bird perks and live influencer Q&As.

  4. Regional Tourism & Multisensory Experiential

    Tailored VIP packages for UAE-Jordan sports travelers (free drinks/meals, court-side seating, souvenir brackets, and customized accreditation bracelets). Elevated on-court atmosphere with live deep house DJ sets, partner discount coupons, and spectator challenges.

Why it worked — Beyond the Court Standard sports events sell court time and tickets. We packaged 40Love as an elite lifestyle festival. By pairing high-stakes athletic competition with deep house soundscapes, hospitality perks for cross-border tourists, and influencer-led cultural buzz, we unlocked major financial backing from Jordan Kuwait Bank and Orange Telecom.
My role & execution Commercial pitch architecture, campaign narrative, 3-phase rollout roadmap, influencer tiering & unit-economics pacing, UAE regional tourism packaging, and on-ground experiential production on the GHS agency team.
