Khalid's Touch — The Trade Marketing Philosophy

High-performing campaigns are not created in an office silo. By personally surveying over 25 retail supermarket chains across Amman and Salt, auditing warehouse stock versus shelf reality, and evaluating competitor facing dominance (Fine, Pampers, Sanita), we turned field data into actionable trade execution.

1. Shelf Audit Before Spend Never run paid traffic to an area where local retailers are suffering from stockouts or poor shelf facing.
2. Experiential Stand Activation Negotiate dedicated standalone promo corners in flagship hypermarkets (City Mall HyperMax) to capture family foot traffic.
3. Direct Representative Alignment Coordinate weekly with on-ground sales reps (Hamdallah, Hassan, Ali) to enforce contract compliance and track shelf shares.
4. Dynamic Price Indexing Audit competitor price ladders across hypermarkets (Cozmo, Safeway, Yaser) to safeguard consumer value and margin velocity.

Lifey × Jordan Breast Cancer Program

A feminine care brand has permission to talk about women's health that most sponsors don't. We used it — tying 10% of Lifey product value to funding early-detection screening for women who couldn't afford it, then carrying the same idea across three very different rooms.

الكشف المبكر ينقذ الحياة

  1. The Mechanic

    10% of Lifey product value directed to the Jordan Breast Cancer Program to fund screening for women unable to pay for it. A purchase became a contribution without asking the shopper to do anything extra.

  2. The Rooms

    Corporate — a staffed booth at King Hussein Business Park. Sport — sponsorship of the Think Pink padel tournament. Retail — the same messaging on pharmacy and supermarket collateral.

  3. The Partner

    Running under the Jordan Breast Cancer Program and King Hussein Cancer Foundation gave the campaign credibility a brand cannot manufacture alone.

Why it worked Cause campaigns fail when the brand is the hero. Here the brand was the funding route and the programme was the message — which is also why the creative stayed simple enough to survive a booth, a banner and a sponsor board without losing anything.
Confirmed Impact Direct screening subsidies unlocked for women across Jordan through pharmacy and retail product contributions during Breast Cancer Awareness Month.

Launching a Chinese luxury sedan into a market that didn't know the badge. Rather than lead with the brand, we led with a number nobody could parse — then answered it on the next board.

  1. Teaser Phase

    ٤٣١ كيلو بدينار — "431 kilos per dinar". Weight per dinar is not how anyone shops for a car, which is exactly why it stopped people. No offer, no brand giveaway.

  2. Reveal Phase

    امتلك الفخامة — own the luxury. 26,000 JOD, or 431 JOD a month with no down payment. The teaser number turns out to be the monthly instalment.

  3. Integrated Platform

    #THE_LUXURY_WITHIN carried the idea into digital channels, dealer activations, and automotive press material.
